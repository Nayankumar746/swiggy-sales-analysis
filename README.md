# swiggy-sales-analysis
- Excel dashboard analyzing 197K+ Swiggy food delivery orders — sales trends, ratings, and city/state performance

## Project Objective
- Swiggy wants an overview of food delivery order performance, so the business can understand sales trends by time, location, and food type, identify top-performing cities and restaurants, and plan better delivery and marketing strategies going forward.

## Questions (KPIs)
- What are the total sales, average rating, average order value, rating count, and total orders?
- How do monthly sales trend from January to August?
- How do daily sales trend across the week?
- What percentage of sales comes from Veg vs Non-Veg food?
- How do sales, ratings, and orders compare across quarters?
- Which 5 cities generate the most sales?
- How does weekly sales trend look over the full period?
- How do sales vary by state?
  
## Process
- Verified the dataset for missing values and inconsistencies across 197,431 rows.
- Built pivot tables in Excel to answer each of the questions above.
- Combined all pivot tables into a single dashboard and added slicers (Months, Category, Restaurant) to make it interactive.
  
## Project Insight
- Veg orders drive about 65% of sales; Non-Veg makes up the remaining 35%.
- Saturday is the strongest sales day (₹7.78M); Sunday is the weakest (₹7.64M) — though the gap between days is fairly small overall.
- Monthly sales dip in February and October and peak around May and August, showing a cyclical pattern rather than steady growth.
- Bengaluru leads all cities in sales (₹5.5M), with Lucknow, Hyderabad, and Mumbai close behind.
- The average rating holds steady at 4.34 across the board, suggesting consistent customer satisfaction regardless of order volume.
  
## Final Conclusion

From this analysis, I found that Veg food is the clear demand driver, making up nearly two-thirds of all sales, so Swiggy could focus promotions and restaurant onboarding around Veg categories. Since Saturday sees the highest sales, running weekend-specific offers could push this further, while weekday incentives could help lift the slightly slower days. Bengaluru's lead in sales makes it a strong candidate for testing new features or restaurant partnerships before rolling them out to other cities. The consistently high rating (4.34) across quarters is a good sign that service quality isn't slipping even as order volume changes. Overall, this project helped me practice using Excel pivot tables and dashboards to turn a large, raw dataset into insights a business could actually act on.
